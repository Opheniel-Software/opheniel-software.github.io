Opheniel Office

Document, spreadsheet, and presentation editors for your desktop.

The suite includes three apps for working with files on your computer.

Applications

Opheniel Docs

Edit and format DOCX documents and export them as PDFs.

Opheniel Sheets

Open and save XLSX workbooks, organize data, and calculate with formulas.

Opheniel Slides

Create and arrange slides and open and save PPTX presentations.

Some advanced formatting and features may change when files are edited and saved.

Availability

Opheniel Office is in development. Public downloads are not yet available. Windows is the current release priority. Mac support is under evaluation.

About

Opheniel Software is an independent project building practical desktop applications. Opheniel Office is its first project.
